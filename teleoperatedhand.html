Teleoperated Hand Project

Project Summary

The Teleoperated Robot Hand System enables users to remotely control a robotic hand in real time to perform fine motor tasks. By wearing a haptic device, the operator can manipulate objects while receiving tactile feedback, simulating the robot hand's physical interactions with its environment. This approach removes humans from life-threatening situations, such as high-voltage repairs, low-oxygen spaces, or radioactive zones, while improving overall efficiency and accessibility.

The system architecture consists of three main components: a wearable device for tracking user motion and providing haptic force feedback, a central communication computer running ROS 2 to manage low-latency data flow, and the robotic hand attached to a multi-DOF arm to accurately execute the operator's commands.
